# Human Communication Differences: Research Pass 2

**Scope:** Neurotypical adults (middle 80-90% of population, excluding clinical populations). Peer-reviewed sources only. Vendor-funded and low-quality sources flagged explicitly.

**Workflow stats:** 107 agents, 25 sources fetched, 58 claims extracted, 25 verified, 15 confirmed, 10 killed, 7 after synthesis.

**Research threads:** Commercial typology tools (MBTI, DISC, etc.), cultural dimensions (Hofstede, Hall), linguistic relativity (Sapir-Whorf), adult attachment style in workplace, socioeconomic/educational background effects, evidence-based communication interventions.

---

## Summary

The peer-reviewed evidence consistently shows that popular commercial frameworks (MBTI, Hofstede VSM) fail basic psychometric validity tests, while attachment theory produces modest but replicated workplace effects. MBTI lacks empirical support for its type structure and fails theoretical validity criteria. Hofstede's VSM 2013 instrument has near-zero or negative internal consistency, making country-level scores unreliable. Personality (Big Five conscientiousness) predicts performance at best modestly (corrected r = 0.19, likely inflated). Adult attachment style shows consistent small-to-moderate associations with workplace relationship quality, engagement, and performance, but workplace attachment operates as a domain-specific construct that does not map directly from romantic attachment research. Linguistic relativity has limited but real empirical support: language influences perceptual discrimination speed for colors, mediated by verbal working memory, though effects are narrow and context-dependent rather than globally reshaping thought.

## Confirmed Findings

### 1. Commercial Communication Frameworks

**Finding:** MBTI fails all three standard scientific theory criteria and has no detectable type structure in score data.
**Confidence:** High | **Vote:** 3-0 (both constituent claims)

Stein and Swan (2019, *Social and Personality Psychology Compass*) found MBTI theory lacks agreement with known facts and data, is not testable, and contains internal contradictions. Separately, Arnau et al. (2003, *Personality and Individual Differences*) applied Meehl's taxometric coherent cut kinetics to MBTI and other Jungian assessments and found no support for discrete underlying types -- preferences manifest as continuous dimensions. A 2025 psychometric synthesis (Erford et al., *Journal of Counseling and Development*, 193 studies) found convergent validity evidence for MBTI sparse, with only 0.5% of career-measure correlations reaching r = 0.50. Vendor claims about team development and conflict management utility are not grounded in valid theory or empirical predictive validity.

**Practical implication:** MBTI typologies ("INTJ," "ENFP," etc.) are not valid categories. Using them for team composition, conflict management, or communication planning produces the impression of rigor without the substance.

**Finding:** Big Five conscientiousness is the strongest personality predictor of job performance, but the corrected correlation of approximately r = 0.19 represents a modest, not strong, effect.
**Confidence:** High | **Vote:** 3-0

A second-order meta-analysis of 54 meta-analyses (n = 554,778) found conscientiousness demonstrated the strongest relationship with performance (corrected r = 0.19), with other Big Five traits ranging from r = -0.12 (neuroticism) to r = 0.13 (openness). A PLOS ONE publication-bias analysis suggests even the 0.19 figure is inflated roughly 30%, with bias-corrected estimates of r = 0.12-0.13. By comparison, general mental ability validity is approximately r = 0.51, making personality a second-tier predictor.

**Practical implication:** Individual-difference frameworks have a low ceiling for predicting workplace outcomes. Personality explains some variance but not enough to be the primary design variable for team communication.

*Sources: [Stein & Swan 2019](https://swanpsych.com/publications/SteinSwanMBTITheory_2019.pdf), [PubMed meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34687041/)*

### 2. Cultural Dimensions

**Finding:** Hofstede's VSM 2013 instrument fails at the measurement level -- internal consistency is near-zero for individualism and negative for power distance, and external validity correlations fall below conventional adequacy thresholds.
**Confidence:** High | **Vote:** 3-0 (all three constituent claims)

Gerlach and Eriksson (2021, *Frontiers in Psychology*, n = 22,863 across 57 countries) reported Cronbach's alpha for the individualism scale at 0.04 (full sample) and 0.13 (subsample), far below the accepted threshold of 0.70. The power distance scale produced alpha = -0.71 (full sample), indicating negative item intercorrelation and structural invalidity. External validity was equally poor: individualism correlated r = 0.23 with Hofstede Insights scores (labeled "inadequate" by the authors) and r = 0.41 with meta-analytic benchmarks (labeled "inconclusive"). A separate Taras et al. (2023, *Journal of World Business*) comparative evaluation independently confirmed VSM has the lowest Cronbach's alphas (averaging 0.43) among competing instruments.

**Important distinction:** These findings invalidate the VSM 2013 *measurement instrument*, not the underlying theory that cultural dimensions exist. The construct (individualism vs. collectivism, power distance, etc.) may capture real variation. The tool used to measure it does not work.

**What remains open:** Hall's high-context vs. low-context model was not covered by any surviving claim in this pass. That thread remains unresolved.

*Sources: [Gerlach & Eriksson 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8056018/)*

### 3. Linguistic Relativity (Sapir-Whorf)

**Finding:** Language influences perceptual discrimination speed for colors where vocabulary differs across languages, and this effect is mediated by verbal working memory, not spatial working memory.
**Confidence:** Medium | **Vote:** 2-1 (one dissenting verifier flagged sample size and generalizability)

Mongolian speakers (who have two distinct blue-hue terms) discriminated between-category blue hues faster than within-category hues. Chinese speakers (one blue term) showed no equivalent advantage. A verbal interference task disrupted the Mongolian between-category advantage while a spatial working memory task did not, implicating verbal mediation. Study: n = 32 per group, single university -- small and not top-tier.

**What this means:** Language shapes the speed and ease of certain perceptual discriminations where lexical granularity differs across languages. It does not globally reshape thought or prevent people from making distinctions their language lacks words for. This is the empirical residue of Sapir-Whorf that survived modern testing.

**Practical implication for communication:** Vocabulary differences matter at the margin for perceptual tasks, but this effect is narrow and context-dependent. It does not support the broader claim that people who "think differently" are linguistically trapped -- just that having a word for something makes you faster at noticing it.

*Sources: [Frontiers in Psychology 2019](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.00551/full)*

### 4. Adult Attachment Style in Workplace Settings

**Finding:** Adult attachment style predicts a range of workplace outcomes including leader-member exchange, supervisor trust, job performance, job satisfaction, organizational commitment, and work engagement, with effects operating partly through trust in supervisor.
**Confidence:** High | **Vote:** 3-0 (all three constituent claims)

Warnock et al. (2024, *Journal of Business and Psychology*, 109 independent samples, n = 32,278) found anxious attachment predicted reduced LMX (r = -0.20) and reduced supervisor trust (r = -0.17), and confirmed indirect effects of both anxious and avoidant attachment on job performance, job satisfaction, organizational commitment, turnover intentions, and LMX through trust in supervisor. Anxious attachment also predicted job stress, turnover intentions, and reduced work engagement. A separate 2023 meta-analysis (Academy of Management Proceedings, 151 samples, n = 36,695) independently corroborated the same directional pattern.

Key qualifications: effect sizes are small (r = 0.17-0.20 range); underlying studies are predominantly self-reported and cross-sectional, which inflates correlations; effects appear conditional on leadership quality and organizational context; anxious attachment overlaps with neuroticism, leaving partial confounding unresolved.

**Finding:** Anxiously attached individuals are perceived more positively in initial team interactions, but this advantage disappears over time as teammates accumulate relational knowledge.
**Confidence:** Medium | **Vote:** 2-1 (small n=26 in Study 2 flagged)

A 2022 *Frontiers in Psychology* study combined a vignette experiment (n = 636) and a 12-week longitudinal team study (n = 26 in 7 groups). Anxiously attached individuals were rated more positively in initial interaction vignettes, but this effect was non-significant in longitudinal team observation.

**Practical implication:** First-impression-based team formation and short-term project assessments may systematically underestimate the relational friction anxious attachment creates over a full project lifecycle. Someone who interviews or onboards well may create more friction at week 8 than week 1.

**Finding:** Workplace attachment is a domain-specific construct -- work-specific attachment dimensions load separately from romantic attachment dimensions in factor analysis.
**Confidence:** Medium | **Vote:** 2-1 (domain specificity, belonging); 3-0 (engagement, performance)

Greskovicova and Lisa (2023, *Frontiers in Psychology*, n = 1,352, two-wave time-lagged) used EFA (KMO = 0.814, four factors explaining 56.8% variance) and found work-specific attachment dimensions loaded separately from romantic attachment. Secure workplace attachment predicted higher belonging scores (3.77 vs. 3.00, d = 0.871, large effect), and modestly higher work engagement (d = 0.328) and general performance (d = 0.318).

**Practical implication:** Romantic attachment research (the larger body of work) does not transfer straightforwardly to workplace prediction. Someone can be a secure workplace colleague and an anxious romantic partner, or vice versa. Treat workplace attachment as its own construct.

*Sources: [Warnock et al. 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10869-024-09960-9), [ODU meta-analysis](https://digitalcommons.odu.edu/psychology_fac_pubs/203/), [Greskovicova & Lisa 2023](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10232801/), [Frontiers 2022](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9190780/)*

## Open Questions (No Surviving Claims)

Three research threads produced no claims that survived adversarial verification. These remain genuinely open.

1. **DISC, Insights Discovery, and other non-MBTI commercial tools.** The surviving evidence only covers MBTI. Whether DISC or Insights have better (or worse) peer-reviewed support is unanswered.

2. **Socioeconomic and educational background effects.** No confirmed claims addressed whether SES or education level predicts communication register flexibility, vocabulary range, or code-switching ability in workplace settings.

3. **What actually works.** The one structured intervention claim in the dataset (a CAD communication workshop) was refuted 0-3. The question of what evidence-based techniques demonstrably improve communication in mixed groups remains open. This is the most practically important gap.

4. **Attachment and personality confounding.** The meta-analyses acknowledge but do not fully resolve overlap between anxious attachment and neuroticism. Incremental validity of attachment style over Big Five traits in workplace settings has not been established with sufficient statistical power.

## Connection to Pass 1 Findings

Pass 1 established: learning styles (VAK) are debunked, cognitive load theory is well-supported, Big Five personality traits predict some outcomes, working memory differences are real and consequential.

Pass 2 adds:

- The commercial framework problem is deep. It's not just VAK -- MBTI and the VSM 2013 Hofstede instrument also fail basic psychometric standards. The common thread: taking continuous human variation and forcing it into discrete categories produces tools that feel useful but don't predict anything reliably.
- Attachment style is the most practically interesting new finding. It's not a typology (no discrete types), it operates on a continuum, it's domain-specific to the workplace, and its effects compound over time in ways that first-impression assessments miss. Effect sizes are modest but the directional findings are well-replicated.
- The "what works" question remains unanswered by both passes. Learning styles don't work; MBTI doesn't work; structured CAD workshops didn't survive verification. The positive case for evidence-based communication intervention is still open.
